About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Exeter and a researcher at the Liverpool Centre for Cardiovascular Science. My work spans ECG analysis, medical image grading, and reasoning and representations in language models.

Research Interests

  • Clinical AI & ECG: Combining ECGs and clinical data for stroke risk prediction, and studying ECG markers of cardiovascular–kidney–metabolic burden.
  • Medical vision: Developing methods for medical image grading and cross-modal learning, including ordinal prediction, text-guided learning and labelled optimal transport.
  • Language & reasoning: Studying how reasoning-step length affects language models and where concepts are encoded across model layers.
